01 — OVERVIEW

What is
Chandrayaan?

Chandrayaan is India's lunar exploration programme developed by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). The name comes from Sanskrit: Chandra means Moon and Yaan means vehicle or craft.

Through orbiters, landers and rovers, the programme studies the Moon's surface, minerals, water-related signatures and environment. Each mission builds on lessons from the previous one.

✦Mission goal
To expand scientific knowledge of the Moon and advance India's space technology.
